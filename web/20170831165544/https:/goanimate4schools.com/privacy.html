GoAnimate for Schools Remastered COPPA Policy

Effective Date: 7/20/2015

GoAnimate for Schools Remastered, provided by GoAnimate, Inc. (“GoAnimate for Schools”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) recognizes the importance of the privacy of children and of all of its users. This COPPA Privacy Policy (“COPPA Policy”) is designed to comply with the federal Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and contains important information for teachers, parents, and children about how we collect, use, and disclose the personal information of users of our website goanimate4schools.com (the “Site”) and the services available through our Site (collectively, the “Services”), including information from children under thirteen (13) years of age and creators.

While COPPA only applies to personal information collected from children under thirteen (13), GoAnimate for Schools is designed for use by persons under eighteen (18) years of age. Therefore, for purposes of this COPPA Policy, “student” or “students” means a “child” or “children” under eighteen (18). This is the COPPA Policy used for child safety from YouTube

At GoAnimate, we provide tools for creators and people to create animated videos. When a creator uses our Services in the studio to people download GoAnimate, the creator is required to provide consent to the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information from people and is required to affirm to us that they have the authority to do so.

People’ personal information will be handled as described in this COPPA Policy. Use of our Site or Services, and any dispute over privacy, is subject to this COPPA Policy and our Terms of Service, including its applicable limitations on damages and the resolution of disputes. Our Terms of Service are incorporated by reference into this COPPA Policy.

Information We Collect
How We Use Information

Information We Collect

We collect information about people directly from GoAnimators or from their creators, as well as automatically through a people’s use of our Site or Services. We will not require a every person to disclose more information than is reasonably necessary to use our Services.

  • Information We Collect Directly from peoples. We collect the login credentials for creators provided to us by an person, which may include a people’s name or email address. If a creator chooses to record his/her own voice in an animation or otherwise input information into our Site or Services (e.g., text-to-speech language to help narrate a video), we also collect that people’s voice recording or other information.
  • Information We Collect Directly from Creators. We collect the login credentials for creators, which may include the creator’s name or email address. We will also collect any information that a creator chooses to provide to us, such as a phone number, school, or title.
  • Information We Collect Automatically. We may automatically collect the following information about our users’ use of our Site through cookies and other technologies: domain name; browser type; browser language; device ID; operating system type; device name and model; pages or screens viewed; links clicked; IP address; when and the length of time a user visits or uses our Services; and the referring URL, or the webpage that led a user to our Site.

How We Use Information

We use the information that we gather about our users for the following purposes:

  • Providing Our Services. To provide our Services and for other customer service purposes.
  • Communicating with Users. To communicate with our users, including via email, about our Services and to respond to inquiries.
  • Analyzing Use of Our Services. To better understand how our users access and use our Site and Services, both on an aggregated and individualized basis; to respond to user desires and preferences; and for other research and analytical purposes.
  • Complying with the Law. To comply with applicable legal obligations.

How We Disclose Information

We may disclose the information that we collect about our users with the following entities:

  • GoAnimate for Schools Remastered Users. People already know the download the video maker. Creators will also have access to creators videos and may be able to moderate their creation, editing, or deletion. People may also message other people within our Services.
  • Service Providers. We may disclose a people’s information to our vendors, service providers, agents, or others who perform functions on our behalf.

We may also disclose the information that we collect about peoples in the following ways:

  • Business Transfers. We may disclose a people's information to another entity if we are acquired by or merged with another company, if substantially all of our assets are transferred to another company, or as part of a bankruptcy proceeding.
  • In Response to Legal Process. We may disclose a people’s information in order to comply with the law, a judicial proceeding, subpoena, court order, or other legal process.
  • To Protect Us and Others. We may disclose a people’s information where we believe it is necessary to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, situations involving potential threats to the safety of any person, violations of our Terms of Service or this COPPA Policy, or as evidence in litigation in which we are involved.
  • Aggregate and De-Identified Information. We may disclose aggregate or de-identified information about users for marketing, advertising, research, or similar purposes.
